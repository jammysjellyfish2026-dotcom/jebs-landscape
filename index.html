Core Service

Professional Mowing

Weekly & biweekly precision cutting with commercial-grade mowers. Sharp blades, perfect stripes, consistent height every visit.

What's Included

  • Commercial-grade rotary mowers (no residential push mowers)
  • Blades sharpened daily for clean cut, not tear
  • Alternating mow patterns to prevent soil compaction
  • Height adjusted seasonally (2.5\" spring, 3.5\" summer, 2.5\" fall)
  • Clippings mulched back into lawn for natural nitrogen
  • Edges trimmed along all hardscapes & beds
  • All hard surfaces blown clean (driveways, walks, patios)
Frequency: Weekly or Biweekly  |   Starts at: $45/visit  |   No contracts — cancel anytime
Included

Trim & Edge Included

Clean edges along driveways, walkways, and beds. String trimming around obstacles. No extra charge — it's part of the service.

What's Included

  • Precision edging along all concrete: driveway, sidewalk, patio, curbs
  • String trimming around trees, posts, AC units, fences, play equipment
  • Bed edges redefined monthly for crisp bed lines
  • Weed-eating in hard-to-reach areas mowers can't access
  • All trimming debris blown off surfaces
  • Zero additional charge — ever
Included Free: With every mowing visit — no upsells, no surprises
